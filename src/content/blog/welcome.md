Most businesses lose 20-40% of their conversion data before it ever reaches their ad platforms. That means your cost-per-lead numbers are wrong, your ROAS is inflated, and you're optimizing campaigns on bad data.

## The Three Places Tracking Breaks

**1. Tag Manager misconfiguration.** Triggers fire on the wrong events, variables aren't populated, or tags are blocked by consent management tools. The fix: audit every tag against actual form submissions, not just pageviews.

**2. Cross-domain leakage.** If your funnel spans multiple domains (landing page → CRM → thank-you page), the session breaks unless you've configured cross-domain tracking correctly. Most setups haven't.

**3. Server-side gaps.** Browser-side tracking is dying. iOS restrictions, ad blockers, and cookie consent mean you're missing 30%+ of events. Server-side tagging through GTM Server or a CAPI integration is the fix — but it has to be set up right.

## What to Do About It

Start with a tracking audit. Map every conversion event in your funnel, then verify each one is actually firing and reaching your ad platforms with the correct parameters.
