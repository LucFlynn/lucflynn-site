# Google Ads for Healthcare / Medical

You're spending $3-5k/month on Google Ads but can't track which campaigns drive actual appointments because HIPAA compliance broke your conversion tracking. Meanwhile, your cost per lead keeps climbing because Google's algorithm is optimizing for form fills instead of patients who actually show up.

## What's Actually Going Wrong

**Your tracking is HIPAA non-compliant and broken**. Most healthcare practices use standard Google Ads conversion tracking that passes patient information directly to Google — a HIPAA violation that also gives you garbage data. I see practices tracking "contact form submissions" when what they actually need is "appointments scheduled" and "appointments attended."

**You're bidding against plastic surgeons for "back pain" keywords**. Healthcare has massive keyword overlap between specialties with wildly different patient values. A chiropractor appointment is worth $200. A spine surgery consultation is worth $15,000. But Google doesn't know that, so you're in bidding wars that make no financial sense.

**Your location targeting is backwards**. I audit healthcare accounts where they're targeting a 25-mile radius around their practice, competing with hospital systems that have $50,000/month budgets. Meanwhile, they're missing the 3-mile radius where 60% of their actual patients live.

**Enhanced Conversions is feeding Google the wrong signal**. Google's machine learning thinks a 22-year-old filling out a form for cosmetic surgery is the same as a 65-year-old booking a cardiology appointment. Your algorithm optimization is training on random form fills instead of your actual ideal patient profile.

## What I Do Differently

I build HIPAA-compliant tracking infrastructure first, then optimize campaigns around actual patient value — not form fills. 

While typical agencies focus on impressions and clicks, I set up server-side conversion tracking that captures appointment scheduling and patient lifetime value without passing PHI to Google. This means we're optimizing for patients who show up and pay, not random website visitors.

I use software to monitor campaign performance 24/7. If your appointment booking tracking breaks or your cost per patient acquisition jumps 40% overnight, you get an automated alert within hours — not a discovery three weeks later in a monthly report.

Every campaign build starts with a geographic analysis of your actual patient data. We target the zip codes where your best patients live, not arbitrary radius circles around your practice location.
