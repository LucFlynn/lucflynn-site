# GA4 Setup & Configuration

Your GA4 setup is broken if you can't trust the numbers in your ad accounts. I see properties every week with 30-50% data loss because someone clicked "create property" and called it done. Event tracking that only fires on desktop. Conversions that don't match what actually happened. Google Ads campaigns optimizing toward ghost data.

Everything downstream — your attribution reports, your audience targeting, your bid optimization — depends on GA4 collecting clean data from day one.

## How It Works

**Week 1: Audit & Planning**
I audit your current setup (if any) and map out your conversion flows. Most sites have 3-5 key events that matter for optimization. We identify those, plus any custom tracking needs specific to your business model.

**Week 2: Implementation**
I configure the GA4 property, set up all tracking through GTM, and implement server-side validation where beneficial. For ecommerce sites, this includes purchase tracking, cart abandonment events, and product view tracking. For lead gen, this covers form submissions, phone clicks, and content engagement.

**Week 3: Testing & Verification**
Real-world testing across devices and browsers. I verify events are firing correctly, conversions are being recorded, and the data flowing to Google Ads matches reality. Any issues get fixed before handoff.

**Week 4: Documentation & Training**
Complete documentation of your setup, plus a walkthrough session where I explain how everything works and what to watch for in your reports.
