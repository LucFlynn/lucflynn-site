# Attribution Platform Setup

Attribution platforms promise to fix your broken tracking. But I've seen too many $500-2,000/month Triple Whale and Hyros setups that are complete garbage because the foundational tracking wasn't configured properly first. You can't build attribution on top of broken pixel data — it's like trying to navigate with a broken compass.

## What You Get

When I set up your attribution platform, you get:

- **Complete platform configuration** — Every tracking pixel, webhook, and integration properly connected and firing
- **UTM and URL parameter mapping** — Your traffic sources properly tagged and mapped to platform attribution models
- **Cross-platform data verification** — Attribution data reconciled against native platform data with acceptable variance ranges
- **Custom conversion event setup** — Platform-specific conversion tracking for your funnel stages and customer lifecycle events
- **Integration testing documentation** — Step-by-step verification that everything is working, plus troubleshooting guides for common breaks
- **Data flow audit** — Complete map of how data flows from visitor action → attribution platform → your dashboards

## How It Works

**1. Foundation Audit (Week 1)**
I audit your current tracking infrastructure before touching attribution. About 70% of the attribution setups I fix were broken because of upstream pixel issues, not the attribution platform itself. No point configuring Triple Whale if your Facebook pixel is only catching 60% of conversions.

**2. Platform Setup (Week 2)**
Configure the attribution platform with your specific business model. This includes setting up custom conversion events, properly mapping your UTM structure, and connecting all ad platforms and analytics tools. I also configure the lookback windows and attribution models that actually make sense for your funnel length.

**3. Integration & Testing (Week 3)**
Connect everything and verify data flow. I run test campaigns and purchases to ensure attribution is working correctly, then cross-check the numbers against native platform reporting. Acceptable variance is typically 10-20% due to attribution modeling differences.

**4. Documentation & Handoff (Week 4)**
You get a complete setup guide, data flow documentation, and troubleshooting playbook. Plus training on how to read the dashboards and what metrics actually matter for your business.
