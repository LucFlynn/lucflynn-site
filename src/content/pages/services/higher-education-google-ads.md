# Google Ads for Higher Education

Your enrollment periods are short, your decision cycles are long, and your current Google Ads setup is probably attributing a $15,000 program enrollment to the last click from "university near me." I've audited 30+ higher ed accounts where they're spending $20k/month but can't tell you which campaigns actually drive quality applications.

## What's Actually Going Wrong

**Your tracking thinks every conversion happens in one session.** Higher education decision cycles span 3-6 months. Students research programs, compare schools, attend virtual info sessions, then apply during enrollment windows. But Google Ads is crediting the final "apply now" click and missing the awareness-stage campaign that actually started the journey.

**You're optimizing for applications, not actual enrollments.** I see higher ed accounts celebrating 200 applications per month while enrollment teams complain about lead quality. Without proper enrollment tracking, Google's algorithm optimizes for people who fill out forms, not people who actually pay tuition.

**Program-level performance is invisible.** Your MBA program might be profitable at $400 CPA while your certificate programs lose money at $150 CPA. But most higher ed accounts I audit have everything lumped together, so profitable programs subsidize wasteful spend on low-value programs.

**Seasonal budget allocation is backwards.** Enrollment windows are predictable, but most accounts spend evenly year-round. I've seen schools blow their entire Q4 budget on November awareness campaigns, then have nothing left for January enrollment pushes.

## What I Do Differently

I build tracking infrastructure first, campaigns second. While other agencies launch campaigns on day one, I spend week one building proper attribution models that connect initial research queries to final enrollment outcomes.

Every setup includes Enhanced Conversions with hashed student data, multi-touch attribution reporting, and program-level revenue tracking. I don't optimize for vanity metrics — I track cost per enrolled student and lifetime value by program type.

My monitoring system catches broken tracking before you lose spend. Automated alerts fire when conversion rates drop, when Enhanced Conversions stops receiving data, or when campaign performance deviates from seasonal baselines.
