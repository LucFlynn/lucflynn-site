# Conversion Tracking Audit

Your conversion tracking is probably broken. I know this because I've audited over 200 accounts, and 80% of them have at least three major tracking issues. Double-counting conversions, missing events, pixels firing multiple times, attribution models that don't match reality — it's a mess out there.

The thing is, everything downstream depends on your tracking. Your ROAS calculations, your attribution reports, your budget allocation decisions — all garbage if the foundation is broken. You can optimize campaigns all day, but if you're measuring the wrong things, you're just getting really good at failing.

## How It Works

**Week 1: Deep Audit**
I get access to your GTM, ad accounts, and analytics. I spend 3-4 hours going through every tag, every conversion event, every attribution touchpoint. I'm looking for the stuff that breaks silently — tags that fire twice, events that don't match between platforms, revenue numbers that don't add up.

**Week 2: Analysis & Documentation**
I map out your entire tracking flow, identify the gaps and overlaps, and quantify the business impact. That "small" double-counting issue? It might be inflating your ROAS by 30%. That missing mobile conversion event? Could be why your mobile campaigns look like they suck.

**Week 3: Implementation Support**
We fix the critical stuff first. I either implement the changes myself (if you give me access) or walk your team through each fix. Every change gets tested in preview mode before going live. No breaking your existing setup.

**Week 4: Verification & Handoff**
We verify everything is working correctly across all platforms. I document the new setup and create monitoring procedures so you can catch issues before they become problems.
