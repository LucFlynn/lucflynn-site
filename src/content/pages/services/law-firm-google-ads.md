# Google Ads for Law Firms

I audit about 30 law firm Google Ads accounts each year, and 80% of them are bleeding money on clicks that never convert to case consultations. The other 20% are converting, but they have no idea which ads actually brought in their $50K personal injury cases versus their $500 traffic tickets.

## What's Actually Going Wrong

**Your call tracking isn't connected to your ads.** You're using CallRail or CallTrackingMetrics to track phone calls, but Google Ads has no idea which keywords and ads generated your highest-value cases. You're optimizing for clicks and form fills while your $200/click personal injury keywords get budget pulled because Google thinks they're "underperforming."

**You're competing on broad match in a $150 CPC market.** I see law firms running "car accident lawyer" on broad match, paying for clicks from people searching "how to become a car accident lawyer" and "car accident lawyer salary." Your daily budget gets torched by 9 AM on irrelevant searches while actual prospects search for you at night and weekends when your budget is exhausted.

**Your landing pages don't match search intent.** Someone searches for "DUI lawyer near me" and lands on your general "Criminal Defense" page that mentions DUI in paragraph three. Your cost per lead looks reasonable at $400, but your lead-to-consultation rate is 15% instead of the 40%+ I see with properly matched landing pages.

**You're not tracking the right conversions.** You've got "Contact Form Submitted" set up as your primary conversion, but 60% of your actual clients call directly from the SERP or your ads. Google's algorithm is optimizing for form fills while ignoring the phone calls that turn into your biggest cases.

## What I Do Differently

**Tracking infrastructure first, campaigns second.** Before I touch your ad copy or keywords, I build complete conversion tracking that connects phone calls, form submissions, and actual case values back to specific ads and keywords. Your Google Ads account knows which search terms brought in your $75K truck accident case versus your $1,200 speeding ticket consultation.

**Software-powered monitoring, not monthly check-ins.** I've built automated systems that alert me within hours when your tracking breaks, your CPA spikes above target, or your impression share drops in key practice areas. Most agencies notice these problems during their monthly review — I catch them the day they happen.

**Practice area-specific campaign architecture.** Your personal injury campaigns run differently than your family law campaigns, which run differently than your criminal defense campaigns. Each practice area gets its own conversion actions, landing pages, ad schedules, and bid strategies based on how those specific clients actually convert.
