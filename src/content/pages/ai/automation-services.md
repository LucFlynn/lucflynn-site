# AI Automation Services: What We Build and What It Costs

If you're spending $100+/day on ads, you have more data than you can realistically look at. Campaign metrics, keyword performance, lead volume, weekly trends — it piles up faster than any one person can process it. The businesses I work with don't need more dashboards. They need something that reads the data and surfaces what matters, on its own.

That's the lane I work in: AI automation for ads, leads, and marketing data. Not customer-service bots, not generic dev work — specifically the layer between your ad accounts and your team.

## What We Build

### Ad Account Chat Bots

Natural-language querying of your Google Ads and Meta accounts. Ask "what were my top 10 keywords last month by conversion volume?" and get an answer in plain English — no logging into the platform, no building a pivot table. I just shipped one for a client with three active accounts (two Google, one Meta). They query all three from a single Slack interface.

This is read-only by design. The bot never touches campaign settings or spend. That's not a limitation — it's the point. See [custom AI agents](/ai/custom-ai-agent) for more on how the read-only guardrails work.

### CRM and Lead-Data Agents

Agents that read your CRM, summarize lead activity, and flag what needs attention. Useful if you're running high-volume lead gen and someone on your team is manually checking "how many leads came in this week, where did they come from, which ones haven't been followed up." That's a task a well-scoped agent handles better than a human.

### AI Reporting — Harness and Fully Automated

Two variants, depending on how much you want to be in the loop:

**Harness (semi-automated):** You drop in the client data, the system generates a report draft in your voice, and you can chat back and forth to edit it. Useful for agencies producing high volumes of client reports — same format, consistent tone, fraction of the time.

**Fully automated:** The bot reads the data on a schedule, writes the email, and sends it without you touching it. I built one that fires every Monday — reads the previous week's performance, identifies which keywords are working, flags trends, and sends the summary directly to the client. Zero manual steps.

### Campaign and Keyword Scoring

A proprietary system that reads account data, scores campaigns and keywords on performance signals, and surfaces which accounts need attention. Built for media buyers managing multiple accounts — instead of manually checking 15 accounts every morning, you see a prioritized list of what needs work. The system catches the things that would otherwise slip through.

### SEO and Website Agents

We're running AI-assisted SEO operations right now — the pages you're reading are part of that. Agents that research keyword clusters, draft content briefs, identify gaps, and track rankings. Website automation that handles the repetitive work in a digital-marketing operation.
